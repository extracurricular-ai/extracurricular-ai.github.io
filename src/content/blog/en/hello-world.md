Every meaningful project starts with a simple question. Ours was: *why is it so hard to just build things with AI together?*

Not research. Not papers. Not demos that live in notebooks. Real tools, shipped in public, built by people who are genuinely curious — not chasing credentials.

That question became a group chat. The group chat became late-night hacking sessions. The sessions became **Extracurricular AI**.

---

## Why we exist

The AI space moves fast. Faster than any one person can keep up with. But the tooling, the communities, and the culture around AI often lag behind — gated behind expensive API credits, closed research groups, or communities that implicitly require you to already know everything before you're welcome.

We think that's backwards.

The best way to understand a technology is to *use* it — to break it, rebuild it, and share what you learned. That's how the best engineers work. That's how the best communities grow.

ExAI is built on that belief.

---

## What we shipped first

Our first public project is **Open Deep Research** — an open replication of OpenAI's Deep Research capability. It runs multi-agent pipelines in the background, streams results in real time, and does it all with a lightweight Preact UI.

We built it fast. We shipped it open. We learned a lot along the way — about agentic systems, about streaming architectures, about what "good enough to ship" actually means.

That's the whole point.

---

## What we believe

We're not trying to be the next AI lab. We're not trying to compete with anyone. We're just trying to create a space where curious builders can show up, build something real, and make it public.

A few things we hold as non-negotiable:

**Everything is open source.** No paywalls, no private repos, no "enterprise edition." If we build it, you can read it, fork it, and improve it.

**Learning happens in public.** We share our mistakes as readily as our wins. A half-finished project with a good writeup is worth more than a polished demo with no explanation.

**Everyone belongs here.** You don't need a CS degree, a job at a big lab, or a thousand GitHub stars. If you're curious and willing to ship, you're one of us.

**Free forever.** Our tools stay free. The community stays open. That's the deal — today and always.

---

## What's coming

We're just getting started. Here's what we're thinking about next:

- More open research replications — there's a lot of interesting work to build on
- Community project proposals — we want ExAI to be a place where *your* ideas get built
- Learning resources — writeups, breakdowns, and post-mortems from everything we ship
- A blog (you're reading it) — where we think out loud about what we're building and why
